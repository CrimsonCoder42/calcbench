## CONSUMER DEFECTS
- calcium_correction: computes Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − albumin) citing Payne 1973; Payne says Ca − albumin + 4.0 (coefficient 1.0); calcium_correction.tsx:50. Severity medium.
