# budget-calculator

FIELDS (form `calform`): income lines salary (80,000/y), pension, investment (1,000/y), otherincome (2,000/y), each
with m|y; taxrate (28 %, flat combined rate); then expense lines (mortgage, property tax, … each m|y).

CLASSIFY: SKIPPED. The page sums income after a flat tax rate and sums expense lines, then compares each category
with suggested shares of income. The sums are arithmetic, and the suggested shares are rules of thumb with no
primary source. Nothing to build or compare. No submissions.
