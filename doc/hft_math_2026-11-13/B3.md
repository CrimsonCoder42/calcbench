## Notes for the lead / reviewer

- Section and equation numbers NOT confirmed against the books (no copy here): BJRL 5th ed. §2.1.5, §3.2.6,
  App. A3.2, §7.1, §8.2.2; Brown & Hwang §4.2; Hamilton eq. [1.1.10]; Lo & MacKinlay equation numbers (cited by
  section and Theorem 1 instead). The formulas themselves are the standard ones and are hand-checked in the tests.
- Imports: num/series now imports num/stats (LinearRegression, reused for AR1Fit and the Hurst slope) and
  num/prob (ChiSquareSurvival for the LjungBox p-value), allowed by the brief's D-009 note. Correlate
  (NUM-SIG-015) is not reused for ACF: series may not import signal, and its normalisation differs.
- New sentinel series.ErrZeroVariance (autocorrelation.go).
- LjungBox uses the mean-centred r_k (as the ACF); the paper's residual r̂_k is uncentred. Equal for residuals
  with zero mean; documented.
- KalmanFilter1D: random-walk model (φ = 1, H = 1) only; no Merge (not defined).
- Tests written, not run (brief D5). Builder checks: gofmt -l clean, go build ./... and go vet on both packages clean.
