# crs_grading → MED-ONCO-001 ASTCTCRSGrade
- Citation: Lee DW, Santomasso BD, Locke FL, et al. ASTCT Consensus Grading for Cytokine Release Syndrome and Neurologic Toxicity Associated with Immune Effector Cells. Biol Blood Marrow Transplant. 2019;25(4):625-638. doi:10.1016/j.bbmt.2018.12.758, PMID 30592986, PMC12180426.
- Locator: Table 2 (ASTCT CRS Consensus Grading) and its footnotes; text "CRS grade is determined by the more severe event: hypotension or hypoxia."
- Transcribed: Fever "Temperature ≥38°C" all grades (not attributable to another cause); after antipyretics or anti-cytokine therapy (tocilizumab, steroids) "fever is no longer required to grade subsequent CRS severity". Hypotension: G1 "None"; G2 "Not requiring vasopressors"; G3 "Requiring one vasopressor with or without vasopressin"; G4 "Requiring multiple vasopressors (excluding vasopressin)". Hypoxia: G1 "None"; G2 "Requiring low-flow nasal cannula or blow-by" (≤ 6 L/min); G3 "Requiring high-flow nasal cannula, facemask, non-rebreather mask, or Venturi mask" (> 6 L/min); G4 "Requiring positive pressure (eg: CPAP, BiPAP, intubation and mechanical ventilation)". Organ toxicities "do not influence CRS grading". Grade 5 = death due to CRS (not computed).
