- Primary: Leonard JC, Harding M, Cook LJ, et al. PECARN prediction rule for cervical spine imaging of children presenting to the emergency department with blunt trauma: a multicentre prospective observational study. Lancet Child Adolesc Health. 2024;8(7):482-490. doi:10.1016/S2352-4642(24)00104-4, PMID 38843852, PMC11261431 (author manuscript; Europe PMC XML returned HTTP 500, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov page read).
- High-risk (four): "Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) score of 3–8 or Alert, Verbal, Pain, Unresponsive (AVPU) scale of U; abnormal airway, breathing, or circulation findings; and focal neurologic deficits" (abstract: "including paresthesia, numbness, or weakness").
- CART-identified (five): "neck pain, altered mental status (GCS score of 9– 14; AVPU scale of V or P; or other signs of altered mental status), substantial head injury, substantial torso injury, and midline neck tenderness"; "Substantial injuries were defined a priori as those warranting inpatient observation or surgical intervention." Rule: "combining the four high-risk factors and the five CART-identified factors". Figure 2 is the imaging algorithm (high-risk → CT; CART only → radiographs; none → no imaging): the function returns the factor group only, not the imaging step.
- Population: "Children <18 years experiencing blunt trauma evaluated at 18 EDs". Group labels in code are descriptive names built from the paper's terms ("high-risk factor", "CART-identified factor").
