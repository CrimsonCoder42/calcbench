# Blue

**Citation**: Blue, "A Portable Fortran Program to Find the Euclidean Norm of a Vector", ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software, 1978, 4(1):15–23, doi:10.1145/355769.355771.

**Taken**: Scaling so that the squares in the Euclidean norm neither overflow nor underflow (implemented here with exact powers of two).

**Used by**: NUM-LINALG-007, NUM-LINALG-017, NUM-LINALG-041, NUM-LINALG-045, NUM-LINALG-046, NUM-LINALG-105.

**Checked**: DOI confirmed on Crossref 2026-11-12. Tests: norms of vectors near 1e300 and of subnormal vectors.
