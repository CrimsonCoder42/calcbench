# stumbl_battle_score → MED-RESP-040 STUMBLScore (PORTED)
- Citation: Battle C, Hutchings H, Lovett S, et al. Crit Care. 2014;18(3):R98. doi:10.1186/cc13873. PMID 24887537, PMC4095687.
- Locator: Table 4 (risk score column); worked example in Results.
- Values: age "1 per additional 10-year increase starting at 10 years of age"; rib fractures "3 per additional rib fracture"; chronic lung disease 5; pre-injury anticoagulant use 4; oxygen saturation "2 per 5% decrease in oxygen saturations starting at 94%". Example: "a 67-year-old (score 6) patient with three rib fractures (score 9), a history of chronic lung disease (score 5) and oxygen saturations of 87% (score 4) would have a final risk score of 24 points". Under-18s excluded. Score → probability of complications: 0–10 13%, 11–15 29%, 16–20 52%, 21–25 70%, 26–30 80%, 31+ 88% (not ported).
- Reading: 94 % is the first scored saturation (90–94 → 2, 85–89 → 4 …); reproduces the example.
- Route: PMC article page. Read 2026-11-12.
